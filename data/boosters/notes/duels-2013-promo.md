# Duels of the Planeswalkers 2013 promotional boosters

The five-card variable portion accompanies a fixed nonfoil PDP13 promo: Primordial Hydra for Xbox, Serra Avatar for Steam, or Vampire Nocturnus for PS3. The sealed products supply that sixth card separately.

[MTGBoosters opens all three platform packs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q45wpKmDO_w). PS3 (1:27–3:03) contains Serra Angel, Krenko's Command, Titanic Growth, Captain's Call and Talrand's Invocation: three commons and two uncommons. Xbox (3:44–4:44) contains Chandra's Fury, Vastwood Gorger, Rancor, Captain's Call and Vedalken Entrancer; Steam (5:06–6:00) contains Servant of Nefarox, Krenko's Command, Rise from the Grave, Vastwood Gorger and Pacifism. Both have four commons and one uncommon. [Contemporary owners](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/boards/671863-magic-the-gathering-duels-of-the-planeswalkers-2013/63208836) independently report both layouts. The author's initial reference to rares is corrected to uncommons.

The model uses the full M13 common/uncommon pools, following the existing M14/M15 Duels base-booster convention. No restricted checklist is established. This is a pool approximation, not proof that every printing has been observed. There are no basic lands, rare/mythic extras or foil slots in the model.

The two layouts receive equal estimated weights, following `3ed-starter.yaml`'s convention for documented layouts with unknown production frequencies. The 50:50 split is not an official rate or an estimate from three opening samples. Layout eligibility is shared across platforms; the sample does not establish platform-specific collation. Within-rarity weights are uniform, also an approximation.

[Wizards' community manager announced the PSN release and six-card redemption pack on June 19, 2012](https://blog.playstation.com/2012/06/19/magic-the-gathering-duels-of-the-planeswalkers-2013-out-today-on-psn/). The other platforms launched June 20. These are 2012 products despite the 2013 branding.
